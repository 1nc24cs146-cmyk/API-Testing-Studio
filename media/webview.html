ApiDiya v0.2.1

Send requests, save collections, switch environments. Secrets are never shown in full in History. New in v0.2: assertions, test suites, import/export, and a local mock server.

Privacy: everything ApiDiya stores stays on this device only — collections, environments, suites, test runs, mocks and history in local editor storage (never synced to a cloud), and saved auth tokens in your operating system's keychain. No account, no cloud, no telemetry. The only network traffic is the request you send, to the URL you typed.
